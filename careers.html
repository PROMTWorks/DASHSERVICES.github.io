Careers at DASH MOBILE SERVICES

More than just one job. Join a growing company where your work can involve different services, new skills, and opportunities to continue building your future with DASH MOBILE SERVICES.

More Than Just One Job

Working for DASH MOBILE SERVICES means being part of a company where your day-to-day work can involve many different services. Depending on your position, training, and the needs of the business, you may work on services such as automotive work, oil changes, cleaning, property services, and other services DASH offers to customers.

You may not know exactly which service you will be performing every day, but you can expect your schedule to remain relatively consistent from week to week while your day-to-day work may change. We believe this gives employees the opportunity to build a variety of skills while continuing to grow within DASH MOBILE SERVICES.

What You Can Expect

DASH MOBILE SERVICES is being built around the idea that employees should be trusted to work rather than have someone constantly standing over them. We want employees who can think for themselves, make educated decisions, use good judgment, and ask for help when they genuinely need it.

As employees gain experience, they may have opportunities to learn additional services and responsibilities and expand their role within DASH MOBILE SERVICES.

Growing With DASH

Our goal is to help employees continue developing their skills while building their future with DASH MOBILE SERVICES. Training may cover automotive services, cleaning, property services, and other areas as the business grows.

As DASH expands, the company may also provide company vehicles and other resources to help employees perform their work without relying entirely on personal vehicles and fuel.

What DASH Expects From You

  • Show up for scheduled shifts and arrive on time.
  • Be available for at least 30 scheduled hours per week.
  • Take your responsibilities seriously and perform the work you were hired to do.
  • Work independently while following DASH policies and safety standards.
  • Be willing to learn new services through proper training.
  • Know your limits and ask for assistance rather than attempting work you are not trained to perform.
  • Treat customers, coworkers, and management with respect.
  • Remain professional even when dealing with difficult customers or stressful situations.
  • Work cooperatively with coworkers without using seniority to avoid responsibilities or mistreat others.
  • Take care of customer property, company vehicles, equipment, tools, and other resources.
  • Communicate with management when an important problem, emergency, absence, accident, or other situation occurs.
  • Be willing to travel to different cities for service calls and, when required by the position, travel outside of South Carolina.
  • Meet the physical requirements of the position, including extended periods on your feet and the ability to lift 50 pounds or more without assistance when required by the position.
  • Represent DASH MOBILE SERVICES professionally at every customer location.

Respect Goes Both Ways

DASH MOBILE SERVICES believes employees deserve to be treated with respect, and we expect employees to give that same respect to customers, coworkers, and management.

DASH may have employees who are older or more experienced than the business owner. That does not change the professional relationship. The owner is responsible for operating DASH MOBILE SERVICES, establishing company standards, making management decisions, and employing the people who work for the company.

We are looking for people who can work professionally within that structure regardless of age or experience.

Customers Come First

Every customer interaction matters. Employees are expected to remain courteous, professional, and respectful regardless of a customer's personality or attitude.

Customers trust DASH MOBILE SERVICES with their homes, vehicles, belongings, property, and private information. Employees are expected to respect that trust and maintain appropriate professional boundaries.

Safety & Responsibility

Getting the job done is important, but getting the job done safely is part of the job.

Employees are expected to use proper safety procedures, follow training, use equipment correctly, and make safety a consideration in every decision they make.

DASH understands that accidents and honest mistakes can happen. Employees are expected to report problems honestly and promptly rather than hiding them. Reckless behavior or intentional disregard of company standards may result in disciplinary action.

Professionalism & Teamwork

You do not have to be best friends with everyone you work with. You do have to be able to work alongside them professionally.

DASH MOBILE SERVICES does not use seniority as an excuse for employees to pawn off responsibilities, bully coworkers, or prevent another employee from learning or participating in the work.

Social Media & Representing DASH

Employees may help promote DASH MOBILE SERVICES through appropriate social media and other content. Customer privacy, company property, employee safety, and confidential information must always come first.

Employees may not use customer property, customer information, or DASH company vehicles as personal content opportunities without proper authorization. Employees are also expected to understand that intentionally using their work connection to damage DASH MOBILE SERVICES or mislead the public about the company is not acceptable, subject to applicable law and protected activity.

Equal Employment Opportunity

DASH MOBILE SERVICES is committed to following applicable federal and South Carolina employment laws and does not make employment decisions based on legally protected characteristics.

The applicable laws may include the South Carolina Human Affairs Law and applicable federal employment laws. Employment decisions are based on legitimate considerations such as qualifications, availability, skills, experience, job requirements, performance, conduct, and the needs of the business, subject to applicable law.

Before You Apply

Please make sure you understand the basic expectations of the position before submitting an application. DASH MOBILE SERVICES positions may require 30 hours or more of weekly availability, travel between cities and, when required, outside of South Carolina, extended periods of standing and physical activity, lifting 50 pounds or more without assistance when required, learning different services, professional customer interaction, reliable attendance, teamwork, and following DASH safety and service standards.

If these expectations do not fit your availability, abilities, or the type of workplace you are looking for, DASH MOBILE SERVICES may not be the right fit.

Our Goal

We are looking for people who want to work, learn, grow, and help us build a company that customers and employees can trust.
