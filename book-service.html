Automotive Booking

Choose your vehicle using DASH's enhanced vehicle selector, then select the exact service you need. Vehicle coverage uses U.S. passenger-vehicle data for model years 1981–2026, with an engine confirmation step before exact fitment or pricing is finalized.

Important: Selecting a vehicle and service begins the booking process. Your appointment is not confirmed until DASH receives the required payment and sends the official confirmation email stating that your payment was received, the requested service is confirmed, and your service date, arrival window, and estimated completion time are set.
