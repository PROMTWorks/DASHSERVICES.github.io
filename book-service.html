Automotive Booking

Select your vehicle and service. Your vehicle information is used to determine service requirements and the estimated service cost.

Engine Air Filter Replacement: DASH will provide and install a new engine air filter appropriate for the selected vehicle. This service does not include diagnosis or other repairs unless separately booked and confirmed.
Your preferred date and arrival time: Please tell DASH when you'd prefer us to come. We will use your preference to schedule your service as close to your requested date and time as possible. DASH ultimately determines the actual service date and arrival time/window based on availability, routing, service requirements, and scheduling. Your preferred time is a request, not a guaranteed appointment time.
Fluid Top-Off: DASH will verify the appropriate fluid and level for the selected vehicle before adding anything. If the requested fluid is not appropriate, the level indicates a different issue, or additional repair is needed, DASH will explain the situation before performing work outside the booked service.
Tire Replacement: Need one tire replaced? Two? Three? All four? DASH lets you book what you actually need. Your vehicle information helps DASH determine the appropriate tire size and service requirements. Tire availability and exact fitment must be confirmed before payment. If the requested tire is unavailable or a different compatible tire is required, DASH will confirm any change with you before charging you for the service.
Payment & confirmation: Your appointment is not confirmed until DASH receives the required payment and sends the official confirmation email stating that payment was received, the requested service is confirmed, and your service date, arrival window, and estimated completion time are set.
