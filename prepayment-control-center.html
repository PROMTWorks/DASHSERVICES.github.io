Payment Verification Workflow
1. Customer books
Booking creates or links the customer/job and invoice.
2. Customer pays before service
Cash App, Venmo, cash or future Stripe payment can be recorded.
3. Owner verifies
Payment is recorded as received and tied to the invoice/job.
4. Dispatch unlocks
Only paid/authorized jobs become dispatch-ready.
Operating Rule
UNPAID = DO NOT DISPATCH

The database now enforces this rule for normal paid jobs. A job cannot move into dispatch-related statuses while payment is required and the dispatch gate is not satisfied.

PAID = READY FOR DISPATCH
