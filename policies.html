Policies & Terms

Please review the applicable DASH MOBILE SERVICES policies before booking or receiving service.

Important: Sending a payment does not by itself confirm a booking. Customers are responsible for following the booking, payment, property-access, and other requirements that apply to their service.

1. Payment, Pricing & Booking Policy

Payment Information

DASH MOBILE SERVICES may maintain records of payments received, including the payment date, amount, booking number, payment method, and applicable transaction information for administrative, accounting, customer-service, and recordkeeping purposes.

When paying through Cash App or Venmo, customers must include “For [Customer Name]” and the applicable DASH booking number in the payment note or description, as instructed by DASH.

Payment Identification

Customers are responsible for providing required payment information accurately and completely. Missing, incorrect, or incomplete payment information may delay identification or application of a payment and may affect booking status, service dates, or scheduling availability.

Payment Confirmation

Sending a payment does not automatically mean that a booking is confirmed. A booking is officially confirmed only after DASH verifies the required payment and completes its applicable booking-confirmation process.

Payment Disputes

If a customer believes a payment was submitted but DASH's records do not show it as received, DASH may request transaction information for review. A payment dispute does not automatically establish that payment was received.

Reversed, Failed, or Disputed Payments

If a payment is declined, reversed, canceled, refunded by the payment provider, disputed, charged back, or otherwise not successfully retained by DASH, it will not be considered valid payment for the booking. The customer may remain responsible for the applicable booking amount, and the booking may be placed on hold, canceled, or otherwise affected until the issue is resolved.

If a payment provider independently reverses or removes a payment previously received by DASH, DASH will notify the customer by email when applicable.

Late or Pending Payment

If the required booking payment has not been received at least two (2) days before the scheduled service date or by the scheduled service date, the booking will be placed into Payment Pending status.

If payment is received after the original scheduled service date has passed, DASH will no longer hold the original appointment date and the customer will be placed on the DASH Wait List for future scheduling. DASH cannot guarantee or provide an estimated service date when a late payment is received.

Original Service Date

DASH does not guarantee that an original service date or appointment time will remain available after a payment deadline has passed. The original date may be offered to another customer or otherwise become unavailable.

Customers may communicate scheduling preferences, but DASH determines the applicable service date.

2. Service Quality, Complaints & Re-Service Policy

Service Complaints

Customers may submit a complaint regarding a completed DASH service and may be required to provide photographs, videos, documentation, service information, or other relevant evidence.

Administrative Review

DASH Admin will review the complaint and available evidence to determine whether the booked service was properly completed according to applicable DASH service requirements and the agreed scope of the booking.

A complaint based solely on a customer's personal preference or disagreement with DASH's established service standards, procedures, or method of performing the service does not automatically qualify for a refund, complimentary re-service, or other compensation.

Evidence

DASH may consider photographs, videos, booking information, service notes, employee reports, customer documentation, and other relevant information. DASH Admin determines the relevance and reliability of submitted evidence.

Service Deficiency vs. Customer Preference

A service will not be considered deficient solely because a customer would have preferred a different appearance, method, result, level of detail, or manner of performance when the service was completed according to DASH standards and the agreed scope.

Complaint Review

Customers should use the designated DASH complaint process and provide sufficient information and supporting evidence. DASH may request additional information when necessary.

DASH Admin's review may take up to fourteen (14) calendar days from the date the complaint and required supporting evidence are received. Customers should not assume that a complaint has been approved, denied, or resolved until DASH provides an official decision.

Approved Re-Service

If DASH Admin determines that the evidence sufficiently establishes that the booked service was not properly completed, DASH may approve a complimentary re-service at no additional charge.

Re-Service Scheduling

An approved complimentary re-service will be scheduled within five (5) to ten (10) days, subject to DASH availability and circumstances affecting scheduling. Customers may communicate scheduling preferences but may not require or select a specific re-service date.

Approved Refund

If DASH Admin determines that a complaint and supporting evidence establish that the booked service was not properly completed, DASH may approve a full refund of the amount paid for the affected booking. Approved refunds are issued from DASH business funds and processed within five (5) to ten (10) days.

3. Service Address & Property Access Policy

Accurate Service Address

Customers must provide the complete and accurate address where the booked service is requested.

Property Access

The service location must be reasonably accessible to DASH personnel at the scheduled time. Customers are responsible for ensuring that DASH personnel can access the property when they arrive.

Locked Properties

DASH personnel will not wait for a locked property, gate, door, building, or other access point to be opened after arrival. Required access must be available when DASH personnel arrive.

If the property cannot be accessed, DASH may determine that the service cannot be performed. Any refund resulting from an access issue will be determined according to applicable DASH policies and the circumstances.

Gate Codes and HOA Access

Customers must provide required gate codes and other necessary access information in the designated Property Notes section when applicable, including for HOAs, gated communities, apartment communities, neighborhoods, or property-managed locations.

Property Notes

Customers should provide gate codes, entry instructions, building information, unit numbers, parking instructions, and other information reasonably necessary to locate and access the service property.

4. Customer Property & Personal Belongings Policy

Customer Belongings

Customers are responsible for securing valuable, fragile, sensitive, or personally important belongings before service begins.

Access to Personal Property

DASH personnel may handle or move customer property only when reasonably necessary to perform the booked service and in accordance with DASH procedures.

Unsecured Items

DASH is not responsible for delays, limitations, or changes to the service resulting from customer belongings that prevent DASH personnel from safely or properly performing the booked service.

5. Damage, Existing Conditions & Pre-Existing Issues Policy

Existing Conditions

Properties, surfaces, furniture, fixtures, vehicles, equipment, landscaping, and other items being serviced may have existing damage, wear, stains, deterioration, defects, discoloration, or other pre-existing conditions.

Pre-Existing Conditions

DASH is not responsible for conditions that existed before DASH personnel began the service.

Documentation

DASH may document existing conditions through photographs, videos, service notes, or other records when appropriate.

Customer Claims

If a customer believes DASH caused damage during a service, the customer may submit a complaint with supporting evidence through the applicable DASH complaint process.

6. Employee Safety & Workplace Conditions Policy

Safe Working Environment

Customers are responsible for notifying DASH of known conditions at the service location that could reasonably affect employee safety or service performance.

Hazardous Conditions

DASH personnel may refuse to enter, pause, modify, or discontinue a service when conditions present a safety concern, dangerous animal, hazardous material, unsafe property condition, or other circumstance that may reasonably place personnel at risk.

Unsafe Service Location

If DASH determines that a location cannot be safely serviced, personnel may leave the property. Any refund or other resolution will be determined according to applicable DASH policies and the circumstances.

7. Service Address Permission & Access Authorization Policy

Service Authorization

Customers must provide accurate information regarding whether the property owner, tenant, HOA, property manager, or other applicable party permits the requested service.

“Yes” Response

Selecting “Yes” represents that the customer believes the requested service is permitted and has a reasonable basis for providing that confirmation.

“Unsure” Response

Selecting “Unsure” may result in DASH requesting additional information, confirmation, or administrative review. Selecting “Unsure” does not guarantee that the service will be performed.

Permission Problems

If DASH determines before or during service that the requested service is not permitted, DASH may decline, pause, or discontinue the service. Any refund or other compensation will be determined according to applicable DASH policies and the circumstances.

8. Weather & Emergency Conditions Policy

Weather Conditions

DASH may delay, reschedule, modify, or discontinue a service when weather conditions make the service unsafe, impractical, or unable to be properly completed.

Emergency Conditions

DASH may take these actions because of emergencies, natural disasters, dangerous road conditions, property emergencies, utility problems, or other circumstances outside DASH's reasonable control.

Scheduling

DASH will determine the appropriate scheduling arrangement based on the circumstances, resources, and future availability. Customers may communicate scheduling preferences, but DASH determines the applicable service date.

9. Employee Representation & Communication Policy

Employee Authority

DASH personnel may communicate with customers regarding the services included in their booking. Employees are not automatically authorized to make binding decisions regarding refunds, cancellations, discounts, complimentary services, policy changes, booking changes, or other administrative matters.

Official Decisions

Customers should rely on official DASH communications for administrative decisions. A statement made by an employee that has not been authorized or officially confirmed by DASH does not automatically change a DASH policy or booking.

Customer Requests

Submitting a request or concern does not guarantee approval. Administrative requests will be reviewed according to applicable DASH policies and procedures.

10. Promotions, Discounts & Coupons Policy

Promotional Offers

DASH may offer promotional pricing, discounts, coupons, or other special offers at its discretion. Each promotion may have its own terms, conditions, eligibility requirements, and expiration date.

Promotional Restrictions

Unless expressly stated otherwise, promotional offers may not be combined with other discounts, promotions, coupons, or special pricing.

No Retroactive Application

DASH is not required to apply a promotion retroactively to a booking that was completed, paid for, or confirmed before the promotion became applicable.

Changes to Promotions

DASH may modify, suspend, or discontinue a promotion subject to applicable promotional terms and obligations.
