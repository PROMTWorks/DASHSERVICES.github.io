# Employee Policies & Procedures

Internal DASH MOBILE SERVICES procedures for employees handling customer-policy situations. Employees must follow the applicable customer-facing policy and use these procedures when a customer issue occurs. Employees may not independently change company policy or promise an outcome outside their authorized role.

## 1. Booking & Payment Handling Procedure

- Verify the booking information before discussing payment or scheduling matters.
- Do not treat a customer statement that payment was sent as proof that payment has been received.
- Follow the approved payment-identification process for Cash App, Venmo, or other approved payment methods.
- Do not promise that a booking is confirmed until the required payment has been verified through the authorized process.
- Document relevant payment problems, including missing booking information, disputed payments, reversals, or failed transactions.
- Escalate unclear or disputed payment situations to the authorized manager or Super Admin.

**Employee limitation:** Employees may not independently declare a payment received, change a payment requirement, or guarantee that a booking will remain scheduled unless their role has expressly been authorized to do so.

## 2. Payment Pending & Late Payment Procedure

When a required payment is late or missing:

1. Review the booking status.
2. Do not promise that the original service date will remain available.
3. Do not promise an immediate replacement date.
4. Follow the approved Payment Pending and Wait List workflow.
5. Escalate unusual circumstances or customer disputes when required.

## 3. Service Complaint & Evidence Procedure

When a customer reports that a service was not properly completed:

1. Remain professional and listen to the customer's concern.
2. Record the applicable booking and service information.
3. Direct the customer to the designated complaint process.
4. Request or preserve appropriate photographs, videos, service notes, or other relevant evidence.
5. Record employee observations without changing or exaggerating the facts.
6. Submit the matter for administrative review.
7. Do not promise a refund, free re-service, or other compensation unless an authorized decision has already been made.

DASH Admin makes the final determination under the applicable customer policy.

## 4. Refund Handling Procedure

Employees receiving a refund request must:

- Listen to the customer's concern.
- Collect the relevant booking information.
- Direct the customer through the approved complaint/evidence process when applicable.
- Explain that the request must be reviewed under DASH policy.
- Avoid promising that the refund will be approved.
- Escalate the request to the authorized reviewer.
- Communicate an approved decision only when that decision has been officially provided.

**Employees may not independently approve, deny, or promise a refund unless specifically authorized.**

## 5. Re-Service Handling Procedure

When a customer requests a complimentary re-service:

- Document the customer's concern.
- Follow the complaint and evidence process when applicable.
- Do not promise that a complimentary re-service will be approved.
- If DASH Admin approves a re-service, follow the assigned scheduling instructions.
- Do not promise a specific re-service date unless DASH has officially assigned it.

## 6. Service Address & Property Access Procedure

Employees must use the service address and property notes provided through the approved booking system.

If access is unavailable:

- Do not force entry.
- Do not bypass a lock, gate, door, or security system.
- Do not enter a property without appropriate authorization.
- Document the access problem.
- Notify the appropriate manager or administrator.
- Follow the approved instruction regarding whether the service can continue.

Employees may not independently promise a refund because access was unavailable.

## 7. Customer Property & Personal Belongings Procedure

Employees should:

- Treat customer property with reasonable care.
- Avoid unnecessary handling of personal belongings.
- Move property only when reasonably necessary to safely perform the booked service.
- Follow DASH service procedures.
- Document significant conditions when required.

If customer belongings prevent safe or proper service, notify the appropriate manager rather than making an unauthorized promise about the outcome.

## 8. Damage & Existing Conditions Procedure

Employees should document relevant pre-existing conditions when appropriate through approved photographs, videos, service notes, or other records.

If a customer claims that DASH caused damage:

1. Remain professional.
2. Do not argue about the final outcome.
3. Document the customer's claim.
4. Preserve relevant evidence.
5. Escalate the matter through the complaint process.
6. Do not promise compensation or accept liability unless specifically authorized.

## 9. Service Permission & Access Authorization Procedure

If an employee learns that the customer may not have permission for the requested service:

- Do not make a legal determination.
- Do not threaten or pressure the customer.
- Do not represent that the service has been approved when it has not.
- Document the information received.
- Escalate the issue according to the authorization procedure.
- Follow management's direction regarding whether service should continue.

## 10. Employee Safety & Workplace Conditions Procedure

Employees are expected to prioritize personal safety.

If a service location presents a reasonable safety concern, employees may pause or discontinue the service and notify the appropriate manager or administrator.

Examples include dangerous animals, hazardous materials, unsafe property conditions, or other circumstances that may reasonably place an employee at risk.

Employees should not continue a service simply because a customer requests or demands that they do so when the employee reasonably believes the situation is unsafe.

## 11. Weather & Emergency Procedure

When weather, emergencies, road conditions, property emergencies, utility issues, natural disasters, or other circumstances outside DASH's reasonable control affect a service:

- Follow DASH operational instructions.
- Prioritize safety.
- Document relevant circumstances when required.
- Do not promise a new service date unless officially assigned.
- Do not promise a refund, discount, or compensation unless authorized.
- Escalate unusual situations to the appropriate manager or Super Admin.

## 12. Customer Communication & Employee Authority Policy

Employees may communicate with customers about the services included in their booking and may receive customer questions, requests, and concerns.

Unless expressly authorized, employees may not independently:

- Change DASH policies.
- Approve or promise refunds.
- Approve unauthorized discounts.
- Promise complimentary services.
- Change official booking terms.
- Guarantee a specific service date.
- Override an administrative decision.
- Create exceptions to company policy.

When a request is outside the employee's authority, the employee should explain that it must be reviewed through the applicable DASH process.

Customers should receive official decisions through authorized DASH communications.

## 13. Promotions, Discounts & Coupons Procedure

Employees must follow the current approved terms for each promotion.

Employees may not independently:

- Create a discount.
- Combine promotions without authorization.
- Apply an expired promotion.
- Apply a promotion retroactively without authorization.
- Change promotional terms for an individual customer.

Customer questions or disputes regarding promotional terms should be handled according to the applicable escalation process.

## 14. Military Discount — In-Person Verification Procedure

DASH MOBILE SERVICES offers a 90% discount only to the individual qualifying service member or veteran who presents valid military or veteran identification and receives the eligible DASH MOBILE SERVICES-controlled service. The discount is not transferable and does not extend to spouses, children, relatives, friends, or other members of the customer's party.

The military discount is available in person only at a physical DASH MOBILE SERVICES location and must not be offered through online booking or online payment. Employees must verify the qualifying person's identification before applying the discount.

The discount applies only to services whose pricing and performance are controlled by DASH MOBILE SERVICES. It does not apply to third-party charges or services, including U-Haul rental charges or other third-party amounts that DASH does not control.

If a non-qualifying family member or other person requests the military discount, employees should politely explain that the discount is limited to the qualifying service member or veteran whose identification is presented and verified. Employees should not argue with, insult, threaten, or attempt to shame the customer.

If a customer becomes abusive, threatening, or materially disruptive after the discount is denied, employees should end the interaction when reasonably necessary for safety and notify a manager or Super Admin. DASH may cancel or decline to perform the affected service and may refuse future bookings when permitted by applicable law and company policy. Any ban or future-service restriction must be based on the customer's conduct or other legitimate business reason, not simply on the fact that the person was not eligible for the discount.

South Carolina law makes knowingly and falsely representing that a person served in the Armed Forces for the purpose of securing a tangible benefit a misdemeanor. Employees should document suspected false representations and escalate them to management rather than making legal threats or attempting to enforce criminal penalties themselves.

**Employee limitation:** Only an authorized employee or manager may apply the military discount. Employees may not create exceptions, extend the discount to family members, or apply it to third-party charges.

## 15. Employee Limitations & Escalation Policy

Employees are responsible for following DASH policies and procedures within the authority assigned to their role.

Employees must escalate matters involving:

- Refund decisions outside their authority.
- Policy exceptions.
- Disputed payments.
- Serious customer complaints.
- Damage allegations.
- Safety concerns requiring management involvement.
- Property permission concerns.
- Requests to change company policy.
- Military-discount eligibility disputes or suspected false representations.
- Any situation where the employee is uncertain about the correct policy or procedure.

Employees must not create a new company rule during a customer interaction. When a policy does not clearly address a situation, the employee should escalate the matter rather than inventing an exception.

## 16. Policy Change Requests

Employees may identify problems, conflicts, or unclear language in DASH policies and may submit the concern through the approved management or HR request process.

Employees do not have authority to edit or publish official DASH policies.

Policy changes are subject to review and approval by the authorized Super Admin.
