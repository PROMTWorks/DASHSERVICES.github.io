Before You Begin

This is a required first step. The Policy Orientation explains the workplace rules and expectations that apply to every DASH employee. It comes before the 15 training modules. Read each section carefully, then complete the short knowledge check and acknowledgment.

1. Attendance & Punctuality

Arrive when scheduled, prepared to work, and ready for the assigned job. If you may be late or unable to report as scheduled, use DASH's required notification process as soon as you reasonably can.

2. Timekeeping

Record your work time accurately using the company's approved timekeeping process. Do not clock in or out for another employee, and do not knowingly submit inaccurate time.

3. Workplace Conduct

Treat customers, coworkers, managers, and members of the public with professionalism and respect. Threatening, abusive, discriminatory, or harassing conduct is not acceptable at work.

4. Customer Privacy & Confidentiality

Customer addresses, access information, photos, contact information, job details, and other non-public information must be protected. Use information only for legitimate DASH work and do not share it with friends, family, or the public.

5. Phones, Photos & Social Media

Use phones appropriately while working. Do not post customer information, private job details, or customer/property photos unless DASH has authorized the use. When in doubt, stop and ask management.

6. Company Property & Equipment

Use DASH vehicles, tools, equipment, records, accounts, and other company property responsibly. Protect company property, report damage or problems promptly, and do not use company property for unauthorized purposes.

7. Safety & Reporting

Follow DASH safety instructions and approved procedures. Report hazards, injuries, damage, unsafe equipment, and other serious job issues through the required process. Safety comes before speed.

8. Following Instructions & Getting Help

Follow legitimate job instructions and the appropriate chain of communication. If you are unsure how to perform a task, encounter a situation outside your training, or believe something is unsafe or inappropriate, pause and ask a manager or the appropriate DASH contact.

9. Training Expectations

Complete required training honestly and take the knowledge checks seriously. DASH employees are expected to understand the material before moving forward. The training program is cross-training: all employees complete the required modules.

10. Important Policy Note

This orientation covers DASH's current workplace expectations. It does not replace job-specific training, safety instruction, or any formal policy document DASH may provide. Where a written DASH policy or direct management instruction gives a more specific requirement, follow that requirement and ask for clarification if needed.
