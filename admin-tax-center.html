How the estimate is calculated

The federal estimate uses the 2026 IRS estimated-tax framework: 92.35% of net self-employment earnings, a 12.4% Social Security component up to the $184,500 wage base after qualifying W-2 wages, a 2.9% Medicare component, the 50% deduction for self-employment tax, and the 2026 federal brackets/standard deductions. South Carolina's 2026 estimated-tax schedule uses 0% below $3,640, 3% minus $109 from $3,640 through $18,229, and 6% minus $656 at $18,230 and above.
