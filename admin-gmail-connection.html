Gmail Connection

Secure DASH Services business email connection. The dedicated company Gmail account is used for both incoming proof matching and DASH automated customer emails.

Required Gmail account
supportdashservices@gmail.com
OAuth callback
roywoofgypiyoobdcrwx.supabase.co/functions/v1/dash-gmail-oauth-callback
Automatic proof matching: Once supportdashservices@gmail.com is connected through Google OAuth, DASH checks the inbox automatically for messages containing a DASH service-request number. Matching proof emails are linked to the pending service request in Supabase. A matching email marks proof as received; it does not automatically approve the proof.
Automated customer email: The same connected company Gmail account is authorized for DASH to send booking confirmations, approved/denied service notifications, proof/restriction notifications, and other authorized transactional messages to customers.
Ready: Connect the dedicated Gmail account through Google's secure OAuth authorization.
If Google shows “403 Access Denied”: make sure supportdashservices@gmail.com is listed as a Test user while the Google OAuth app is in Testing, the Gmail scopes used by DASH are declared in Google Auth Platform → Data Access, Gmail API is enabled, and this exact callback is listed under the OAuth client's Authorized redirect URIs.
DASH requests the Gmail capabilities needed for the current integration:
  1. Gmail read/compose/send access for automatic proof matching and authorized DASH customer emails
  2. Google account email identity so DASH can verify the required company account
  3. Offline access so the background proof matcher and scheduled customer emails can continue without requiring an administrator to remain signed in
The matcher never uses the Gmail password and does not automatically approve customer proof.
