Accessibility

DASH MOBILE SERVICES is committed to providing a website experience that is clear, convenient, and accessible to as many customers as possible. We want customers to be able to access information about our services, understand the options available, and interact with our website without unnecessary barriers.

Our Commitment

We aim to make the DASH MOBILE SERVICES website easy to navigate and understand by focusing on:

  • Clear and readable content that is organized in a straightforward manner.
  • Consistent navigation to help customers find services, information, and support.
  • Descriptive headings and page titles that make information easier to locate.
  • Straightforward language that helps customers understand services and website features.
  • Accessible customer information so customers can easily find important contact and service details.
  • Ongoing improvements as we identify opportunities to make the website easier to use.

Website Accessibility

We recognize that customers may access our website using different devices, browsers, technologies, and accessibility tools. We strive to maintain a website that works well across commonly used devices and provides information in a clear and usable format.

While we continue to improve the website, some pages, features, or third-party services may not provide the same level of accessibility in every situation. When we become aware of an issue, we will make reasonable efforts to review and address it.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing information, navigating the website, or using a particular feature, please contact DASH MOBILE SERVICES Support.

When contacting us, it can be helpful to provide the page or feature you were trying to use and a brief description of the difficulty you experienced. This allows us to better understand the issue and work toward providing an appropriate way for you to access the information or assistance you need.

Continuing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing part of the customer experience we are building at DASH MOBILE SERVICES. As the website, services, and technology continue to develop, we will continue looking for ways to make our information and online services clearer, easier to use, and more accessible to our customers.
