Payment, Pricing & Booking Policy

Payment Records & Payment Identification

DASH may maintain records of payments received, including the payment date, amount, applicable booking number, payment method, and transaction information, for administrative, accounting, customer-service, and recordkeeping purposes. When submitting payment through Cash App or Venmo, customers must include “For [Customer Name]” and the applicable DASH booking number in the payment note or description, as instructed by DASH. This information is required to help DASH accurately identify and apply the payment to the correct customer and booking.

Payment Identification Errors

If a customer fails to include the required customer name and booking number in the Cash App or Venmo payment note, DASH may be unable to immediately identify or properly apply the payment to the customer's booking. Any delay in identifying or applying a payment resulting from missing, incorrect, or incomplete payment information may affect the customer's booking status, service date, or scheduling availability.

Payment Responsibility

The customer is responsible for ensuring that all required booking payments are submitted accurately, completely, and in accordance with the payment instructions provided by DASH. DASH is not responsible for delays, errors, or other consequences resulting from a customer's failure to follow the payment instructions provided at the time of booking.

Payment Confirmation

DASH will determine when a payment has been successfully received and properly identified based on the applicable Cash App or Venmo transaction records. Customers will be notified through the applicable DASH booking confirmation process once their payment has been verified and their booking has been officially confirmed.

Payment Disputes

If a customer believes a payment was submitted but DASH's records do not show the payment as received, the customer may be required to provide relevant transaction information so DASH can review the matter. A payment dispute does not automatically establish that payment was received or require DASH to treat the booking as paid.

Payment Reversals or Failed Transactions

If a payment made to DASH is later reversed, refunded by the payment provider, declined, disputed, charged back, or otherwise not successfully retained by DASH, the payment will not be considered valid payment for the booking. The customer may remain responsible for the applicable booking amount, and the booking may be placed on hold, canceled, or otherwise affected until the payment issue is resolved.

If Cash App or Venmo independently reverses, cancels, or otherwise removes a payment that was previously received by DASH, the customer will receive an email notification stating that the payment was reversed or canceled by the applicable payment provider.

Payment Pending & Late Payment

If the required booking payment has not been received by DASH at least two (2) days before the scheduled service date or by the scheduled service date, the booking will be placed into a “Payment Pending” status. If DASH subsequently receives the required payment after the originally scheduled service date has passed, the original appointment date will no longer be held, and the customer will automatically be placed on DASH's “Wait List” for future scheduling.

DASH cannot guarantee or provide an estimated service date at the time a late payment is received. If the customer subsequently requests to cancel the booking instead of remaining on the Wait List, the applicable DASH customer cancellation policy will apply.

Payment Does Not Guarantee Immediate Scheduling

Receipt of a late payment does not guarantee that DASH will immediately schedule or perform the requested service. Once a booking has entered the “Payment Pending” status and the original service date has passed, scheduling will depend on DASH's future availability and confirmation of a new service date.

No Reservation of Original Service Date

DASH does not guarantee that the original requested service date or appointment time will remain available when payment is received after the applicable payment deadline. The original date and time may be offered to another customer or otherwise become unavailable.

Payment and Booking Confirmation

A payment being sent to DASH does not, by itself, guarantee that a booking has been accepted or confirmed. A booking is considered officially confirmed only when DASH has verified the required payment and completed its applicable booking confirmation process.

Service Quality, Complaints & Re-Service Policy

Service Complaints and Administrative Review

Customers may submit a complaint regarding a completed DASH service and may be required to provide photographic evidence, documentation, or other relevant proof supporting the complaint.

DASH Admin will review the complaint and available evidence to determine whether the booked service was actually not completed in accordance with the applicable service requirements, or whether the complaint is based solely on a customer's disagreement with DASH's established service standards, procedures, or method of performing the service.

A complaint based solely on a customer's preference or disagreement with how DASH performed a service will not qualify for a refund, free re-service, or other compensation.

If DASH Admin determines that the customer's evidence sufficiently establishes that the booked service was not properly completed, DASH may approve a full refund of the amount paid to DASH and provide the customer with a complimentary re-service.

The final determination regarding whether a complaint qualifies for a refund or complimentary re-service is made by DASH Admin.

Any approved refund will be issued from DASH business funds and processed within five (5) to ten (10) days, and any approved complimentary re-service will be scheduled within the same five (5) to ten (10) day timeframe, subject to DASH's scheduling availability.

Evidence and Review Standards

DASH Admin may consider photographs, videos, booking information, service notes, employee reports, customer-provided documentation, and other relevant information when reviewing a service complaint. DASH Admin may determine the reliability and relevance of the evidence provided and is not required to accept a customer's claim solely because a complaint or supporting evidence has been submitted.

Customer Preference vs. Service Deficiency

A complaint will not be considered a service deficiency solely because the customer would have preferred a different method, appearance, result, level of detail, or manner of performance than the service provided by DASH, when the service was completed in accordance with the applicable DASH service standards and the agreed scope of the booking.

Complaint Submission

Customers may submit a service complaint through the designated DASH complaint process and should provide sufficient information and supporting evidence for DASH Admin to review the matter. DASH may request additional information or documentation when necessary to evaluate the complaint and determine the appropriate outcome.

Complaint Review Timeframe

DASH Admin will review submitted service complaints and available supporting evidence within a reasonable period of time. The review period may vary depending on the nature of the complaint, the amount of information provided, the availability of relevant service records, and other circumstances affecting the review.

DASH's review may take up to fourteen (14) calendar days from the date the complaint and any required supporting evidence are received. Customers should not assume that a complaint has been approved, denied, or otherwise resolved until DASH provides an official decision.

Approved Re-Service

If DASH Admin determines that a customer's complaint and supporting evidence sufficiently establish that the booked service was not properly completed, DASH may approve a complimentary re-service. The re-service will address the service deficiency identified during the review and will be performed at no additional charge to the customer.

Re-Service Scheduling

When a complimentary re-service is approved, DASH will determine and provide the customer with a re-service date within five (5) to ten (10) days, subject to DASH's availability and any circumstances that may reasonably affect scheduling.

Customers may communicate scheduling preferences, but may not require or select a specific re-service date. The re-service date assigned by DASH will be the date on which the complimentary re-service is scheduled to occur.

Approved Refund

When DASH Admin determines that a complaint and supporting evidence sufficiently establish that the booked service was not properly completed, DASH may approve a full refund of the amount paid by the customer for the affected booking. Any approved refund will be issued using DASH business funds and processed within five (5) to ten (10) days.

Service Address & Property Access Policy

Accurate Service Address

Customers must provide DASH with the complete and accurate address where the booked service is requested to be performed. DASH will use the service address provided during the booking process for scheduling and service purposes.

Property Access

The service address must be reasonably accessible to DASH personnel at the scheduled time of service. Customers are responsible for ensuring that DASH personnel can access the property when they arrive.

Locked Properties

DASH will not wait for a locked property, building, gate, door, or other access point to be opened after DASH personnel arrive at the service location. The required access must be available at the time DASH personnel arrive.

If the property cannot be accessed when DASH personnel arrive, DASH may determine that the service cannot be performed and will not service the address during that appointment. Any refund resulting from the inability to access the property is not guaranteed and will be determined according to the applicable DASH policies and circumstances of the booking.

Gate Codes and HOA Access

If the service address is located within an HOA, gated community, neighborhood, apartment community, or other property where a front entrance gate requires a gate code or other access information, the customer must provide the required access information during the booking process in the designated “Property Notes” section.

This requirement applies when the applicable HOA, property, or community permits DASH to perform services at the service address. Failure to provide required gate or access information may prevent DASH from accessing and servicing the property, and any resulting refund is not guaranteed.

Property Notes

Customers should use the “Property Notes” section during the booking process to provide relevant information necessary for DASH personnel to locate, enter, or access the service address, including gate codes, access instructions, building information, unit numbers, parking instructions, or other information reasonably necessary to complete the service.

Customer Property & Personal Belongings Policy

Customer Belongings

Customers are responsible for securing valuable, fragile, sensitive, or personally important belongings before DASH personnel begin the booked service. Customers should not leave items in areas where they may interfere with the safe and proper performance of the service.

Access to Personal Property

DASH personnel are authorized to handle or move customer property only when reasonably necessary to perform the booked service and in accordance with DASH's established service procedures.

Unsecured Items

DASH is not responsible for delays, limitations, or changes to the service resulting from customer belongings or personal property that prevent DASH personnel from safely or properly performing the booked service.

Damage, Existing Conditions & Pre-Existing Issues Policy

Existing Conditions

Customers understand that properties, surfaces, furniture, fixtures, vehicles, equipment, landscaping, and other items being serviced may have existing damage, wear, staining, deterioration, defects, or other pre-existing conditions before DASH begins the service.

Pre-Existing Conditions

DASH will not be responsible for damage, conditions, defects, deterioration, staining, discoloration, or other issues that existed before DASH personnel began performing the booked service.

Documentation

DASH may document existing conditions through photographs, videos, service notes, or other records before or during the performance of a service when DASH determines that documentation is appropriate.

Customer Claims

If a customer believes that DASH caused damage during a service, the customer may submit a complaint with supporting evidence for review according to the applicable DASH complaint and service review policies.

Employee Safety & Workplace Conditions Policy

Safe Working Environment

DASH personnel must be provided with a reasonably safe environment in which to perform the booked service. Customers are responsible for notifying DASH of known conditions at the service location that could reasonably affect the safety of DASH personnel or the performance of the service.

Hazardous Conditions

DASH personnel may refuse to enter, pause, modify, or discontinue a service when conditions at the service location present a safety concern, hazardous condition, dangerous animal, hazardous material, or other circumstance that DASH determines may reasonably place DASH personnel at risk.

Unsafe Service Location

If DASH determines that a service location cannot be safely serviced, DASH may end the service and require DASH personnel to leave the location. Any refund resulting from an unsafe service condition will be determined according to the applicable DASH policies and circumstances of the booking.

Service Address Permission & Access Authorization Policy

Service Authorization

Customers must provide DASH with accurate information regarding whether the property owner, tenant, HOA, property manager, or other applicable party permits the requested DASH service to be performed at the service address.

Customer Confirmation

When the booking process asks whether the customer has permission for DASH to perform the requested service at the service address, the customer must select the response that accurately represents their knowledge of the applicable property permissions.

“Yes” Response

If a customer selects “Yes,” the customer is representing to DASH that the requested service is permitted at the service address and that the customer has a reasonable basis for providing that confirmation.

“Unsure” Response

If a customer selects “Unsure,” DASH may require additional information, confirmation, or administrative review before determining whether the service can be performed at the service address. Selecting “Unsure” does not guarantee that DASH will perform the service.

Permission Issues

If DASH determines before or during the service that the requested service is not permitted at the service address, DASH may decline, pause, or discontinue the service. Any refund or other compensation will be determined according to the applicable DASH policies and circumstances of the booking.

Weather & Emergency Conditions Policy

Weather Conditions

DASH may delay, reschedule, modify, or discontinue a service when weather conditions create circumstances that DASH determines may make the service unsafe, impractical, or unable to be properly completed.

Emergency Conditions

DASH may delay, reschedule, or discontinue a service because of emergencies, natural disasters, road conditions, property emergencies, utility issues, or other circumstances outside DASH's reasonable control.

Scheduling

When a service is affected by weather or an emergency condition, DASH will determine the appropriate scheduling arrangement based on the circumstances, available resources, and future availability. Customers may communicate scheduling preferences, but DASH will determine the applicable service date.

Employee Representation & Communication Policy

Employee Authority

DASH personnel may communicate with customers regarding the performance of the services included in the booking; however, DASH personnel are not automatically authorized to make binding decisions regarding refunds, cancellations, discounts, complimentary services, changes to company policies, or other administrative matters unless DASH has expressly authorized them to do so.

Official Decisions

Customers should rely on official DASH communications for decisions regarding cancellations, refunds, re-services, scheduling changes, payment matters, and other administrative decisions. Statements made by DASH personnel that have not been officially authorized or confirmed by DASH Admin do not automatically change the applicable DASH policies or the customer's booking.

Customer Requests

Customers may communicate requests or concerns to DASH personnel during a service; however, submission of a request does not guarantee that the request will be approved. Administrative requests will be reviewed and handled according to the applicable DASH policies and procedures.

Promotions, Discounts & Coupons Policy

Promotional Offers

DASH may offer promotional pricing, discounts, coupons, or other special offers at its discretion. Promotional offers are subject to the specific terms, conditions, eligibility requirements, and expiration dates established by DASH for each individual promotion.

Promotional Restrictions

Unless DASH expressly states otherwise, promotional offers may not be combined with other discounts, promotions, coupons, or special pricing arrangements.

No Retroactive Application

DASH is not required to apply a promotional offer retroactively to a booking that was completed, paid for, or confirmed before the promotion became applicable.

Changes to Promotions

DASH may modify, suspend, or discontinue a promotional offer at its discretion, subject to any specific terms or obligations applicable to the promotion.
