DASH Policies

Payment, Pricing & Booking

DASH's official payment methods may include Cash App and Venmo, as designated by DASH. Customers are responsible for ensuring payment is sent to the correct official DASH payment account.


Customers are responsible for verifying that the Cash App or Venmo account receiving their payment exactly matches the official DASH payment information displayed on the DASH website.


Payment sent to an incorrect, unauthorized, or unofficial account is not considered payment to DASH. The customer remains responsible for the full final booking amount until payment is successfully received by DASH.


If payment is not received by the applicable deadline, the booking may enter Payment Pending status. If payment is received after the original service date, the customer will be placed on the DASH Wait List.

Service Quality, Complaints & Re-Service

Customers may submit a complaint regarding a completed DASH service and may be required to provide photographic evidence, documentation, or other relevant proof supporting the complaint.


DASH Admin may consider photographs, videos, booking information, service notes, employee reports, customer-provided documentation, and other relevant information when reviewing a service complaint.


A complaint based solely on a customer's preference or disagreement with how DASH performed a service does not automatically qualify for a refund or complimentary re-service.


DASH's review may take up to fourteen (14) calendar days.


When a complimentary re-service is approved, DASH will determine the re-service date within five (5) to ten (10) days, subject to availability.


Any approved refund will be processed within five (5) to ten (10) days.

Service Address & Property Access

Customers must provide the complete and accurate service address.


The service address must be accessible when DASH personnel arrive.


DASH will not wait for a locked property, building, gate, door, or other access point to be opened after DASH personnel arrive.


If a gated community, HOA, apartment community, or other property requires a gate code or access information, customers must provide that information in the Property Notes section during booking.

Customer Conduct & Service Interaction

Customers may communicate reasonable requests or preferences; however, customers may not continuously direct, supervise, interfere with, or otherwise control how DASH personnel perform the service.


DASH personnel must be permitted to perform services according to DASH's established standards, procedures, safety requirements, and professional judgment.


DASH may end service when conditions become unsafe, threatening, abusive, or otherwise inappropriate.

Employee Safety & Workplace Conditions

DASH personnel must be provided with a reasonably safe environment in which to perform the booked service.


DASH may refuse to enter, pause, modify, or discontinue a service when conditions present a safety concern or hazardous condition.

Weather & Emergency Conditions

DASH may delay, reschedule, modify, or discontinue a service when weather or emergency conditions make the service unsafe, impractical, or unable to be properly completed.


DASH will determine the appropriate scheduling arrangement based on circumstances, available resources, and future availability.

Employee Representation & Communication

DASH personnel are not automatically authorized to make binding decisions regarding refunds, cancellations, discounts, complimentary services, changes to company policies, or other administrative matters unless expressly authorized by DASH.


Customers should rely on official DASH communications for decisions regarding cancellations, refunds, re-services, scheduling changes, payment matters, and other administrative decisions.

Promotions, Discounts & Coupons

DASH may offer promotional pricing, discounts, coupons, or other special offers at its discretion. Promotional offers are subject to the terms and conditions established by DASH for each promotion.
