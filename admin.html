Manager Maintenance Workflow

Manager can submit maintenance

Upload maintenance report, receipt, photo, or other proof for oil changes, tires, repairs, inspections, and similar work.

Maintenance mileage: entered by manager as part of the maintenance record.

Official mileage protection

Manager cannot directly edit official vehicle mileage. The mileage update becomes available only after a maintenance record and proof are submitted.

After submission, the maintenance mileage record is locked from manager editing. Corrections require Super Admin action.

Super Admin can upgrade a manager at any time after a confirmation warning. The change will later be recorded in the security log.

Super Admin → Manager

Super Admin can downgrade an account just as easily. The affected account cannot change its own role.
