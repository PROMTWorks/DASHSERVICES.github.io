Employee Performance Policy

This page is for Super Admin/Owner use only. Managers and employees must not receive access to these ratings, thresholds, recommendations, or company-impact analysis.

Privacy rule: Employee performance scoring and replacement recommendations are confidential owner-management information.

Fireable Performance Threshold

60%

Employees with an overall performance rating of 60% or less are classified as “Fireable — Owner Review”.

Important: This is an owner review flag, not an automatic termination. The system will never fire, deactivate, or schedule termination of an employee automatically.

How the owner dashboard should interpret ratings

81–100% · Performing Well

61–80% · Performance Concern / Monitor

0–60% · Fireable — Owner Review

The rating should be based on documented performance data such as attendance, quality, callbacks, customer feedback, productivity, training, equipment/material care, and job contribution. The owner makes the final employment decision.
