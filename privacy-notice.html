Customer Privacy

Privacy Notice

DASH MOBILE SERVICES respects the importance of customer privacy and is committed to handling information responsibly. This Privacy Notice explains the types of information DASH MOBILE SERVICES may receive through its website and customer-service processes, how that information may be used, and how this notice may change as the business and its services develop.

This notice is intended to provide general information about DASH MOBILE SERVICES' current approach to customer information. It should be reviewed and updated as necessary to reflect the company's final privacy practices, systems, and legal requirements before the website begins routinely handling real customer information.

Information We May Receive

Depending on the website feature or service a customer uses, DASH MOBILE SERVICES may receive information such as:

  • Customer name and contact information.
  • Email addresses and telephone numbers.
  • Booking and service-request information.
  • Service addresses or other location information provided for a requested service.
  • Vehicle or service-related information when necessary to provide a requested service.
  • Payment-related identification or transaction information provided through applicable payment services.
  • Messages, questions, feedback, and other communications submitted to DASH MOBILE SERVICES.
  • Information provided when customers use forms, booking tools, or other website features.

The specific information collected may vary depending on the service or feature being used.

How Information May Be Used

Information received through the website or customer-service process may be used to:

  • Process and manage service requests and bookings.
  • Communicate with customers regarding requested services.
  • Provide and coordinate services.
  • Respond to questions, requests, and customer-support inquiries.
  • Maintain appropriate business and service records.
  • Process or support applicable payments and transactions.
  • Improve website functionality, services, and the overall customer experience.
  • Meet applicable business, administrative, and legal requirements.

DASH MOBILE SERVICES will seek to use customer information for legitimate business and service-related purposes consistent with how the information was provided.

Information Sharing

DASH MOBILE SERVICES may use third-party providers or services to support website functionality, communications, bookings, payments, hosting, or other business operations.

When third-party services are involved, those providers may have their own privacy policies and terms governing how information is handled. Customers should review the applicable policies of third-party services when appropriate.

DASH MOBILE SERVICES may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, legal processes, or legitimate business requirements.

Information Security

DASH MOBILE SERVICES recognizes the importance of protecting customer information and intends to use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the systems involved.

However, no website, electronic communication method, or information-storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Customers should avoid submitting sensitive information through website features unless the website specifically requests that information through an appropriate and secure process.

Customer Communications

Information submitted through contact forms, booking requests, email, or other customer-service channels may be retained as necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain business records, or address customer-support needs.

Customers should provide only information that is reasonably necessary for the service or request they are submitting.

Privacy Updates

This Privacy Notice may be updated as DASH MOBILE SERVICES' website, services, technology, and business operations develop. Changes may be posted on this page, and the updated notice will reflect the company's current approach to handling information.

Before the website begins routinely handling real customer information, DASH MOBILE SERVICES intends to review and further develop this notice so that it accurately reflects the company's formal privacy practices, technology providers, data-handling procedures, retention practices, customer rights, and applicable legal requirements.
