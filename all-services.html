Automotive Services

Vehicle maintenance is one of the many everyday services DASH Services can provide. Our goal is to make basic automotive care more convenient by bringing select services directly to the customer, while maintaining the same focus on professionalism, safety, and quality that applies to every DASH service.

Your vehicle stays where it is. We come to you. When available for your vehicle and location, you can have routine automotive services performed at your home, workplace, or another approved location instead of making an extra trip just to keep your vehicle maintained.

When DASH Arrives

Once your service is booked, DASH prepares for the specific vehicle and service you selected. When our DASH service professional arrives, they will confirm the vehicle and service before beginning the work.

Customers are expected to be present at the service location before the service begins, remain available during the service, and be present when the service is completed. This gives you an opportunity to communicate with our service professional, ask reasonable questions, and verify that the requested service was completed.

Our service professionals follow DASH procedures, safety requirements, and service standards. Customer presence does not mean a customer can require work outside the booked service or ask the service professional to ignore company procedures.

At completion, the service professional will communicate what was completed and give you an opportunity to verify the work and raise reasonable concerns.

You booked the service. We prepare for it. We come to you. We do the work. You get to see the result.

If Something Unexpected Happens

A vehicle may be different from the information provided during booking, an unexpected issue may be discovered, or the requested service may require something DASH is not prepared or authorized to perform.

Our service professionals are not expected to guess, improvise beyond their training, or perform work they cannot safely and reliably complete. They will explain the situation and contact DASH management when necessary.

If an unexpected situation affects the scope or price of the service, the service professional will not independently decide to charge you more money. Employees cannot create their own prices, offer unauthorized discounts, or promise free services.

If the situation can be safely resolved within the booked service, DASH may continue. If it cannot, DASH will communicate the appropriate next step.

Important: If the vehicle at the appointment is different from the vehicle entered during booking, DASH may still be able to complete the service when the service is verified as appropriate and safe. The internal documentation and approval process for those situations will be handled through DASH's operating procedures.

Tell the Customer. Don't Pressure the Customer. Let the Customer Decide.

DASH Services does not believe every service call should become a sales opportunity.

If a service professional notices something the customer should know about—such as a damaged dipstick, a tire that is losing air, or another legitimate concern—they should communicate that information honestly and professionally.

That does not mean the service professional should pressure the customer into buying another service or product. The customer has already told DASH what they want. We provide the service they requested.

We will tell you what we notice. What you do with that information is your decision.

DASH does not intentionally use a customer's service call to subconsciously pressure them into spending more money.

Service Completion & Payment

DASH requires payment before an employee is dispatched to the service location. Customers should not expect to wait until the service professional arrives to pay.

Submitting a service request does not automatically mean the appointment is confirmed. First, you will receive confirmation that your request was received. DASH will then provide instructions for completing payment.

Your booking is not confirmed until DASH has received your required payment and you receive the official confirmation email.

The confirmation email will state that your payment was received and that everything is set for the requested service. It will include the service, confirmed service date, scheduled arrival time or arrival window, and estimated time needed to complete the service.

Service Quality & Customer Satisfaction

At DASH Services, completing the service is only part of the job. How the service is performed matters even more.

Our service professionals are expected to treat every customer, vehicle, home, property, and service location with respect and care. They should arrive prepared, act professionally, communicate clearly, and perform the service according to DASH procedures and standards.

We want customers to feel that they received a service that was professional, thorough, respectful, and worth what they paid for.

We understand that not every service will be perfect and that unexpected situations can happen. What matters is that our service professionals make a genuine effort to perform the work correctly, safely, and to the highest reasonable standard possible.

If a customer has a reasonable concern about the service, we want them to communicate it so legitimate concerns can be addressed fairly.

Customer satisfaction does not mean service professionals are expected to ignore safety requirements, company procedures, or reasonable limitations simply to make someone happy. DASH will always prioritize safety, proper procedures, and doing the job correctly.

We don't just want to complete your service. We want you to feel good about choosing DASH Services.
