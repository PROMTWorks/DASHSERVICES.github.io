Lawn & Property Care

DASH Services provides convenient lawn and basic property-care services for residential customers. Services can be requested individually or combined depending on the needs of the property.

Lawn Mowing

Keep your lawn looking clean, neat, and well maintained with routine mowing services from DASH Services. Our lawn mowing service is designed to maintain the overall appearance of your yard while providing a clean finish after each visit.

Service may include:

  • Front and back yard mowing
  • Trimming around accessible obstacles
  • Basic edging along applicable areas
  • Grass clipping cleanup
  • General cleanup of the immediate mowing area

Service requirements may vary depending on the size, condition, accessibility, and layout of the property.

Weed Removal

Manual removal of unwanted weeds from accessible areas including garden beds, mulched areas, walkways, around structures, and other suitable areas.

Mulch Installation

Installation and spreading of mulch in existing landscape beds and suitable outdoor areas, including basic bed preparation, leveling, and cleanup.

Decorative Rock Installation

Enhance the appearance of your outdoor space with decorative rock. Services may include landscape-bed preparation, removal of existing rock or landscape material, placement and spreading of new rock, rock border installation, basic leveling, and cleanup after installation. Available materials may include river rock, pea gravel, crushed stone, and other suitable landscape materials depending on availability and project requirements.

Yard Cleanup

General outdoor cleanup for properties that need additional maintenance or attention, including leaves, small branches and sticks, grass clippings, yard debris, landscape-bed cleanup, and general outdoor cleanup.

Trimming & Edging

Finishing work to create a cleaner, more maintained appearance around walkways, driveways, landscape beds, and accessible obstacles.

Seasonal Yard Cleanup

One-time cleanup services for spring, fall, and other seasonal needs, including leaf removal, branch and debris removal, overgrown-area cleanup, and landscape-bed cleanup.

Property Maintenance

Basic outdoor property-maintenance services for customers who need help keeping their property maintained between larger projects. Services may include routine cleanup, landscape-bed maintenance, trimming, and other basic outdoor maintenance depending on the property and requested work.

Service availability may vary based on property size, condition, materials required, weather, equipment needs, access to the work area, and the specific work requested.
