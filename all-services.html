All Services

DASH Services is built around making everyday services easier. We come to the customer when the service is available for the customer's location, so you don't always have to spend your own time, fuel, or vehicle mileage getting routine things done.

DASH Services is currently exploring the possibility of offering U-Haul rental services. If you need a U-Haul truck, trailer, or other rental equipment, let us know. Your request will simply tell DASH that there is a customer looking for this service.

This is not a booking for a U-Haul rental. DASH Services will review your request and, if appropriate, contact you to discuss your needs and help determine the best way to assist you.

When DASH Arrives

Our DASH service professional confirms the vehicle and service before beginning work.

Customers are expected to be present before, during, and after the service so they can communicate with the service professional and verify the requested work was completed.

DASH procedures, safety requirements, and service standards always apply.

Payment & Confirmation

Payment is required before dispatch. A service request is not a confirmed appointment until DASH receives payment and sends the official confirmation email with the confirmed service, date, arrival window, and estimated completion time.
