DASH Services provides convenient lawn and basic property-care services for residential customers. Services can be requested individually or combined depending on the needs of the property.

Lawn MowingKeep your lawn looking clean, neat, and well maintained with routine mowing services from DASH Services. Our lawn mowing service is designed to maintain the overall appearance of your yard while providing a clean finish after each visit. Service may include front and back yard mowing, trimming around accessible obstacles, basic edging along applicable areas, grass clipping cleanup, and general cleanup of the immediate mowing area. Service requirements may vary depending on the size, condition, accessibility, and layout of the property.
Weed RemovalManual removal of unwanted weeds from accessible areas including garden beds, mulched areas, walkways, around structures, and other suitable areas.
Mulch InstallationGive your landscape beds a clean, finished appearance with professional mulch installation from DASH Services. We can help prepare existing beds and evenly distribute new mulch throughout the designated areas. Service may include preparation of existing landscape beds, removal of minor weeds and loose debris, installation and spreading of mulch, basic leveling and even distribution, defining or refreshing landscape-bed edges, and cleanup of the immediate work area. DASH Services may work with different types and colors of mulch depending on availability and the customer's project requirements. Service requirements may vary depending on the size and condition of the landscape beds, amount of mulch required, accessibility, existing materials, and overall project requirements.
Decorative Rock InstallationEnhance the appearance of your outdoor space with decorative rock. Services may include landscape-bed preparation, removal of existing rock or landscape material, placement and spreading of new rock, rock border installation, basic leveling, and cleanup after installation. Available materials may include river rock, pea gravel, crushed stone, and other suitable landscape materials depending on availability and project requirements.
Yard CleanupGeneral outdoor cleanup for properties that need additional maintenance or attention, including leaves, small branches and sticks, grass clippings, yard debris, landscape-bed cleanup, and general outdoor cleanup.
Trimming & EdgingFinishing work to create a cleaner, more maintained appearance around walkways, driveways, landscape beds, and accessible obstacles.
Seasonal Yard CleanupOne-time cleanup services for spring, fall, and other seasonal needs, including leaf removal, branch and debris removal, overgrown-area cleanup, and landscape-bed cleanup.
Property MaintenanceBasic outdoor property-maintenance services for customers who need help keeping their property maintained between larger projects. Services may include routine cleanup, landscape-bed maintenance, trimming, and other basic outdoor maintenance depending on the property and requested work.
Service availability may vary based on property size, condition, materials required, weather, equipment needs, access to the work area, and the specific work requested.

When DASH Arrives

Our DASH service professional confirms the vehicle and service before beginning work.

Customers are expected to be present before, during, and after the service so they can communicate with the service professional and verify the requested work was completed.

DASH procedures, safety requirements, and service standards always apply.

Payment & Confirmation

Payment is required before dispatch. A service request is not a confirmed appointment until DASH receives payment and sends the official confirmation email with the confirmed service, date, arrival window, and estimated completion time.
