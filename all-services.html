Lawn & Property Care

Property MaintenanceKeep your outdoor property looking clean, organized, and maintained with ongoing or occasional property-maintenance services from DASH Services. This service is designed for customers who need help with general outdoor upkeep between larger landscaping projects or routine lawn services. Service may include general outdoor property cleanup, landscape-bed maintenance, weed and debris removal, trimming and edging, removal of leaves, sticks, and other small yard debris, basic upkeep of accessible outdoor areas, refreshing and maintaining existing landscape areas, and general cleanup following maintenance work. Property Maintenance can be requested as a one-time service or as part of ongoing property upkeep, depending on the customer's needs. Service requirements may vary depending on the size, condition, layout, accessibility, and specific maintenance needs of the property.
