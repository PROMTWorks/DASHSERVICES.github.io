Role & Permission Rules

Employee

  • View own weekly published schedule.
  • Clock in/out only as themselves.
  • View own authorized HR/payroll/time information.
  • Submit sick-time, day-off, emergency, and other requests.
  • Submit time-correction requests without editing punches.
  • Cannot edit schedules, time records, or protected HR/payroll records.

Manager

  • Create and publish employee schedules.
  • Must acknowledge the schedule-lock policy before publishing.
  • Cannot edit or verbally/paper override a published schedule.
  • Cannot see other employees' exact clock-in/out or payroll records.
  • Can see their own HR/payroll/time information but cannot edit it.
  • Must forward employee requests to Super Admin and cannot independently approve/deny them.
  • No company financial, banking, business-credit, or HR Portal access.

HR

  • Access separate HR Portal only within assigned HR permissions.
  • May manage authorized HR workflows and records.
  • No company financial, banking, business-credit, or Super Admin settings by default.
  • Regular managers and employees cannot enter the HR Portal.

Super Admin

  • Full employee, HR, scheduling, time, payroll, request, and permission authority.
  • Only role allowed to approve/decline post-publication schedule changes.
  • Only role allowed to approve/decline time corrections and change protected time records.
  • Only role with company financial and business-credit visibility.

Employee Request Control

Mandatory routing: Every employee sick-time, day-off, emergency, or other request must remain visible in the workflow. Management cannot suppress, skip, or independently reject a request. The required path is Employee → Management → Super Admin. Final approval or decline belongs to Super Admin.

Employee Record Structure

Profile

Name, contact information, job title, hire date, employment status, and internal employee ID.

Access & Permissions

Employee, Manager, HR, and Super Admin roles with controlled access to portal sections and actions.

Assignments

Assigned bookings, service dates, service windows, vehicles, and equipment.

Service History

Completed jobs and work activity connected to customer records.

Scheduling

Managers build schedules. Published schedules become official and locked; only Super Admin can approve later changes.

Time Clock

Employees clock themselves in/out. Exact employee time records are restricted to Super Admin.

Documents

Controlled employee documents area with appropriate role restrictions.

HR Portal

Separate restricted HR workspace for Super Admin and authorized HR employees only.

Audit Activity

Important employee, schedule, request, permission, and correction actions will be recorded for accountability.
